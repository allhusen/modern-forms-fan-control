Your Apple TV sends Home commands through this Mac. Closing Fan Control makes these Home accessories unavailable. The fan’s existing schedule stays on the fan.

Having trouble pairing?

Allow Local Network access if macOS asks. For the first pairing, put the iPhone and Mac on the same network. Your Apple TV also needs to reach this Mac for automations.

If your IoT network is isolated, HomeKit needs local discovery (mDNS) and TCP port 51826 between the Apple devices and this Mac. No router settings have been changed.
